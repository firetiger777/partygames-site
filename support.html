FAQ

Do I need the internet to play?
Not for the pass-and-play games (The Scale, Can't Say, What am I?, Alien Language): they run offline on one phone. The online games need an internet connection on every phone in the room.
How do the online games work?
Open Online games, pick a game and tap PLAY. One person hosts and gets a 4-letter room code; everyone else taps Join and types that code, or opens the host's invite link. Then the host starts the game. Play in the same room or over a video call.
How many players?
Pass-and-play games: solo or two teams (Red vs Blue) sharing one phone. Online games: most start at 2 players; Balderdraw and Detective need 3, Keyphrase needs 4. The lobby shows when a game is ready to start.
Someone can't join my room. What should I check?
Make sure everyone has the latest version of the app (a room made on a different version shows an "update the app" message), that the code is typed exactly, and that the game hasn't started yet: new players can only join from the lobby.
Do I need an account?
No accounts, no sign-up. The online games only ask for a nickname.
Why does "What am I?" use motion?
That game uses the phone's tilt so you can tilt down to score a word and up to pass. Motion is read live on your device and never stored or sent anywhere.
Are there ads or in-app purchases?
No ads, and no in-app purchases: every game is included.
How is my data handled?
Pass-and-play games send nothing anywhere. Online games share your nickname and what you enter only with the players in your room, and delete it when the room closes. See our Privacy Policy.
