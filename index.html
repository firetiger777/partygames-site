Can't Say That!

Our first game
The online forbidden word game.

Describe the word on your card to your team without saying any of the words underneath it. The other team is watching, and they will catch you. Host a room, share a 4 letter code, and everyone joins from their own phone.

  • 2 to 8 players in two teams, Red against Blue
  • Play in the same room or in different cities
  • No account, no sign up, just a nickname
  • Free, with no ads and no interruptions
  • English and Portuguese (Brazil)
