FAQ

Do I need the internet to play?
No. Party Games runs fully offline on one device — there's nothing to connect to.
Do I need an account?
No accounts, no sign-up. Open the app and play.
How many players?
It's a pass-the-phone game for a group. Play solo or as two teams (Red vs Blue), over 1–5 rounds.
Why does "What am I?" ask for motion access?
That game uses the phone's tilt so you can tilt down to score a word and up to pass. Motion is read live on your device and never stored or sent anywhere.
Is there any cost, or ads?
The app is free with no ads and no in-app purchases.
How is my data handled?
We collect nothing. See our Privacy Policy.
