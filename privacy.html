PARTY GAMES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 22, 2026
Party Games does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. No accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no servers. The app works fully offline.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect any personal information about you. Specifically:

  • We do not ask you to create an account or provide a name, email, or identifier.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not collect device identifiers, location, contacts, photos, or usage data.
  • The app does not send any information off your device.

Information stored on your device

To make the app work, a small amount of data is saved locally on your device only and is never transmitted anywhere:

  • Your sound on/off (mute) preference.
  • In-progress game state (teams, rounds, scores), so a game can be resumed if the app is closed mid-session.

You can erase this data at any time by deleting the app.

Device sensors and permissions

  • Motion & orientation (accelerometer): The game "What am I?" reads the phone's tilt so you can tilt down to score a word and up to pass. This motion data is used live, on your device, only while you are playing that game. It is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • The app does not use the microphone, camera, location, or contacts.

Children's privacy

Party Games is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children. Because we collect no personal information, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how the app handles data, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above.
