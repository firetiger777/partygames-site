PARTY GAMES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026
Party Games has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The pass-and-play games run entirely on your device. When you play one of the online games, the nickname you type and what you enter during that game are synced through our game server and shared only with the players in your room. Room data is deleted when the room closes.

Pass-and-play games

The Scale, Can't Say, What am I? and Alien Language are played on a single phone. They work offline and send nothing off your device.

Information used to run an online game

The online games (for example Can't Say Online, Draw-It Online, Keyphrase and Detective) let everyone join a room from their own phone. To make that work, the app sends a small amount of gameplay data to our realtime game database:

  • The nickname you enter. It is free text you choose, it is not verified, and it is shown only to the other players in your room. It does not have to be your real name.
  • What you enter during the game. Depending on the game, that can be a short clue, an answer, the text of a card you write, a vote, or the lines of a drawing. It is shown only to the players in your room.
  • Live game state. The room code, teams, scores, turns, and whether each player is connected. This keeps every phone in the room in sync.
  • A random player id. The app creates a random id the first time it runs and keeps it on your phone. It is used as your seat in a room so you can reconnect to the same seat if your connection drops. It is not your device's advertising identifier and is not linked to who you are.

This data exists to run your game session and for nothing else:

  • It is visible only to people in your room. Rooms are private; joining requires the 4-letter room code or an invite link from the host.
  • It is deleted when the room closes (when the game ends or the players leave).
  • It is not linked to your identity. There are no accounts, and we do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, or anything that ties a nickname to a person.
  • It is never used for advertising, profiling, or analytics, and it is never sold or shared with anyone.

Service provider

Online room data is synced through Firebase Realtime Database, a hosting service operated by Google LLC, acting as our data processor. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Google's own privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy. We do not use Firebase Analytics, Google Ads, or any other Google data products.

Invite links

A host can share an invite link to their room through the phone's share sheet. The link only contains the game and the room code. If you install the app from an invite link, the app checks once, on its first launch, for that room code so it can fill it in for you: on Android it reads the install referrer from Google Play, and on iPhone it looks at the clipboard (iOS shows a paste notice when it does). The code is used on your device only, to join that room.

What we do NOT collect

  • No accounts, sign-ups, emails, or passwords.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access.
  • No purchases: every game in the app is included.

Information stored on your device

A small amount of data is saved locally on your phone only: your sound on/off preference, in-progress game state so a game can be resumed if the app closes, your random player id, your last room so you can reconnect, and whether an invite has already been handled. Deleting the app erases all of it.

Device sensors and permissions

  • Motion & orientation (accelerometer): "What am I?" reads the phone's tilt so you can tilt down to score a word and up to pass. This motion data is used live, on your device, only while you play that game. It is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • The app does not use the microphone, camera, location, or contacts.

Children's privacy

Party Games has no accounts, no chat, and no public matchmaking. In the online games, the only player-provided data is what players enter inside a private room, and it is deleted when the room closes. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this policy and the "Last updated" date above.
